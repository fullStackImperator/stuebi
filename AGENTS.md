# Open questions about content

When you put content on the site that you cannot verify yourself — an old source, an unconfirmed fact, a photo
with unclear rights, wording you made up — add one entry to `docs/offene-fragen.md`: what is unclear, the source,
and the file and field it lives in. Tell the user in your reply as well. Delete entries once they are settled.
